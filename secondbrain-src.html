STEP 1 / 4

Build the vault.

Open Obsidian → make a new vault → create these folders. Numbers keep them in order. Each folder answers one question.

📁 My Second Brain/
├─ 000 Home — your dashboard. what matters right now
├─ 100 Me — who you are. goals, values, story
├─ 200 Timeline — what happened, by date. your life log
├─ 300 Knowledge — things you learn
├─ 400 Projects — one file per thing you're building
├─ 500 People — friends, family, who matters
└─ RAW — the inbox. drop anything here, sort later
◈

Feeling lazy? Start with 3: Me, Projects, RAW. You can grow the rest later. A small vault you actually use beats a perfect one you don't.

STEP 3 / 4

Give your AI the rulebook.

Create one file in your vault that tells the AI how to behave. Copy this to start — change it to fit you:

# rulebook.md — my AI's instructions

You are my second brain.

1. Read my files first. Before answering,
   check my goals, projects, and timeline.
2. Remember what you learn. When I tell you
   something new about my life — save it to a file.
3. Log the days. Big day? Write it to the Timeline.
4. Be honest with me. No hype. Real answers.
Claude Codename it CLAUDE.md — it reads it automatically
ChatGPT / Claude appmake a Project and add your files + rules
Any other AIpaste the rulebook at the start of a chat

Talk to it every day.

This is the secret. A second brain is not built in one day — it grows. Every chat, every note, every logged day compounds.
