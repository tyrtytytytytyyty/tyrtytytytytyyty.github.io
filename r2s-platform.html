V1 Polish · Ramble2Script

Platform Selector & Smart Ramble Parser

Same idea — your ramble becomes a script — but now formatted for whatever platform you're posting to: YouTube, TikTok, Reels. Same input, different scaffolds. TikTok output is hook-heavy and short; YouTube preserves more setup and pacing.

The parser also got smarter. It walks the transcript looking for restart cues — "so basically what I'm saying is…" — and surfaces the cleanest version of each idea, dropping the filler and tangents before generation even starts.
