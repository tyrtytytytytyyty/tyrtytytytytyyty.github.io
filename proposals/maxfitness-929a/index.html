Prototype controls (for Tyrese)

The AI text line runs on Tyrese's phone number. These commands only work when he texts them from his own number; anyone else who sends them gets nothing.

Command cheat sheet

  • max fitness onEvery unknown number that texts gets the Max Fitness line. Turns itself off after 4 hours.Example: max fitness on · custom timer: max fitness on 2 (2 hours)
  • max fitness offKill switch. Effective on the very next text.
  • max fitness statusOn or off, time left, and how many numbers have roles.
  • role <phone> <customer|current|employee|owner> <description>Tells the line who a number is. The description is everything the bot knows about that person; it never invents more. Phone can be typed with or without dashes, spaces or +1.Example: role 706-555-1234 employee general manager, works 9-5 Mon-Fri, oversees front deskExample: role 7065551234 current Premium member, wants to freeze in December
  • role <phone> clearRemoves the role (also: clear <phone>).Example: role 706-555-1234 clear
  • whoami <phone>Shows what the line thinks that number is, and whether it routes to Max Fitness.Example: whoami 706-555-1234
  • rolesLists every number with a role.
  • mf-op sim <phone> <question>Dry run: the answer comes back to Tyrese, nothing is sent to that number.Example: mf-op sim 706-555-1234 how much is a membership?

How the demo works

  1. Tyrese texts himself max fitness on.
  2. The GM texts Tyrese's number with any question ("how much is a membership?"). She gets the automated Max Fitness line: an intro, then real answers from the club's public info.
  3. Tyrese texts role <her number> employee general manager, works 9-5 Mon-Fri. Same number, new role: she asks "what hours am I working?" and it answers from that description only.
  4. Switch her to current and ask to freeze or cancel: it explains the policy and hands the account action to the owner.
  5. Switch her to owner, then text as a customer from another phone with something urgent: the escalation lands on her phone.
  6. Off-topic texts ("tell me a joke") get one fixed reply and cost nothing. When done: max fitness off.

Every reply is checked before it sends: no invented prices, links or phone numbers, no account changes promised, and nobody sees another person's information. Conversations are logged encrypted on Tyrese's Mac.
