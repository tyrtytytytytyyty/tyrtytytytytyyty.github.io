Your text line, start to finish.

One phone number that answers parents in seconds, day or night, books trial classes, and hands you anything that needs a person. Here's exactly how it runs.

What a parent sees

  1. They find you and drop their number.On the trial class form (number only), or they just text the line from Instagram, Google or your site.
  2. They get a text back in seconds, not "within 24 hours".From your gym's line, any hour, including after you close. Same Head Coach voice as your site's assistant. It asks the child's name and age.
  3. It puts them in the right class.Age 7 → Ninjas 6–8, with the price and class length from your own schedule, then offers real trial times.
  4. The trial is booked in the same thread.Name and email for the confirmation, a reminder the day before, and your front desk gets a heads-up.
  5. Families you already have get recognized.If the number matches a family in your list, it knows them ("Mason's still in Thursday Ninjas") and only ever shows that family their own info.

It answers on its own

  • Class prices, ages and lengths
  • Trial classes, start to booked
  • Birthday parties: price, guests, weekends, deposit
  • Camps, hours, address
  • Your FAQ: dress code, can parents watch, class sizes, make-ups

It hands you the rest

  • Billing, refunds, cancellations
  • Injuries or complaints
  • "Can I talk to someone?"
  • Anything it isn't sure about. It never makes up an answer: it says it'll check with the team and texts you the conversation.

You and your staff

  • Text the line: "who's trialing today?"
  • Staff text for their own schedule and nothing else
  • Every conversation is logged so you can check what was said

You stay in control

  • One text turns it off; messages come to you like normal texts
  • It only replies. It never blasts or markets on its own
  • Names and numbers are masked before anything reaches the AI
  • Your data is kept separate from every other business

What we need from you

  1. Which classes take trials.We start from your live iClassPro schedule; you tell us which slots are open to trials and how many per class.
  2. Anything your FAQ doesn't cover.Waivers, the trial price (if any), party age rules (your site lists both 5+ and 6+).
  3. Who gets the hand-offs.Your number, the front desk, and when each one should get pinged.
  4. Optional: your family list.Parent phone, child, class. This is what lets it recognize current families.

Live within 2 weeks of getting the four items above.
