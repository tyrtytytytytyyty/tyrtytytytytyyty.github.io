01 · Where it runs

No Mac on your end. Nothing to install.

Your customers write in the way they already do. Every message lands on one server I run, gets answered there and goes back out. You don't buy hardware, and neither does anyone you sell this to.

  • Email

    Your existing support address.

  • Website chat

    A small chat box on your site.

  • Facebook Messenger

    Where Tactical Traveler already gets messages.

  • Text

    Business texting needs carrier registration first, so it comes after the others.

02 · Privacy

The AI only ever sees blanks.

  1. 1

    A message comes in

    It lands on my server, exactly as the customer wrote it.

  2. 2

    Names and numbers come out

    A small AI model on my own machine swaps out names, emails, phone numbers and member IDs. The question stays.

  3. 3

    The answering model works blind

    It reads the question and writes the reply without ever knowing who asked.

  4. 4

    Real details go back in

    My server fills the blanks back in and sends the reply. Nobody outside saw who it was.

02 · Privacy, tested

Tested on 600 messages it had never seen.

A separate AI wrote fake customer messages for both businesses, full of names, numbers and typos. The blanking code was locked before each round, then scored once.

216 / 217phone, card, member and cert or policy numbers hidden
0numbers sent to the AI in the last four rounds
~5 msto blank a message, on my own machine

The one miss was a cert number spelled out in words ("nineteen oh four A five…"). Names are harder, since people write them every way possible. They land between 87% and 97% depending on the round. Anything that does slip through only goes to an AI provider that keeps nothing (see below).

03 · Separation

Each business gets its own lock and its own key.

My Dive Locker and Tactical Traveler each get separate storage, encrypted with AES-256 under a separate key. One key can't open the other business's data. Add a third brand later and it gets a third key.

04 · What's protected

Straight answer on what's protected, and what isn't.

Covered
  • A stolen or lost drive
  • A leaked backup
  • The AI companies seeing who your customers are
  • The AI provider keeping anything: it runs on OpenAI's GPT-6 Luna through zero-data-retention endpoints only
  • One brand's data showing up in another's
Handled another way

To answer a message, the running server has to read it. So the server itself is what I lock down: only I can get in, it stays updated, and conversations are kept only as long as we agree on.

05 · How it grows

Three stages. Each one moves more of it in-house.

  1. Stage 1 · Build and test

    On hardware I already own

    The blanking model runs on my machine. The answering model is rented. We test with real questions before any customer sees it.

  2. Stage 2 · Go live

    A dedicated server

    A machine that does nothing but this, on around the clock. This is where your customers start using it.

  3. Stage 3 · Fully in-house

    Everything on my hardware

    The answering model moves onto my own machines too. At that point nothing about your customers leaves my servers at all.

Live test

Try to break it on our call.

I'll send you a chat link. Type anything you want, including made-up names, phone numbers and member numbers. On my screen you'll watch each message get blanked, go to the AI, come back and get filled in, in real time.
