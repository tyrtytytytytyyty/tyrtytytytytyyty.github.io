V0 Generator · Ramble2Script

Voice-to-Script Generator

I had a problem writing my scripts, so I built this — it takes my ramble and turns it into a script. Voice memo or pasted text in, polished hook + outline + body out. Whisper handles the transcription, Llama 3.3 70B (via Groq) extracts the actual point I was trying to make and rewrites it sharply.

The product call worth flagging: it doesn't try to preserve my exact phrasing. It surfaces the intent and rewrites it cleanly. That's what most creators actually want when they ramble — not a transcript, a script.
