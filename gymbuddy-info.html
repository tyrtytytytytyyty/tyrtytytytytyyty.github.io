A training log that stays in the conversation.

Log the work you do, keep your place between sets, and look back at the progress you choose to record.

01 / 04

* Simulated conversation

01

Log a completed set.

Text the exercise, reps, and stated weight—or mark it bodyweight. GymBuddy saves the completed set you report, not every movement in the background.

02

Keep your place between sets.

After a set is logged, GymBuddy can start one rest timer and text you when it ends. Use your own interval or turn the rest timer off.

03

Look back at what you recorded.

Ask about a recent workout or a lift's history. Comparisons use the completed sets you logged and only appear when there's enough comparable history.

04

Bring more of training into the thread.

Food photos return macro estimates, not measurements. Short lifting videos are sampled for approximate reps and a small number of confident form observations.

Do I need to download an app?

No separate GymBuddy app is required for the current membership. You use it through iMessage on an iPhone.

After checkout, Tyrese contacts you personally to start setup. This is not an instant automated activation.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime, by texting “cancel” to GymBuddy. Cancel during the 7-day trial and you won’t be charged. Review the full terms before subscribing.

No. It is an AI fitness companion, it can make mistakes, and it is not medical care. For pain, injuries, or a medical concern, talk with a qualified professional.
