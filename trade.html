Trading · Brainrot WORLD

PvP Trade System

A real-time PvP trading layer for Brainrot WORLD. Two players each fill their offer slots; updates replicate live so both sides see the proposed deal as it changes. Acceptance triggers server-side ownership validation and a transactional inventory swap — if either side fails, the trade rolls back atomically.

Architected the entire service: trade UI, slot replication, offer-accept flow, server-authoritative validation, and the atomic transfer pattern. Designed to survive the edge cases that break naive trading systems — disconnected partners, slot manipulation, and partial-state failures.
