$ ./faq


what is this
a small set of scripts. you put them on a usb drive, run one, and it downloads a lightweight ai model and runtime onto the drive. after that you chat in a terminal with no internet.


is it really free
yes. no account, no card.


what computer do I need
8 GB RAM runs the tiny and ~3B brains. the ~8B brain wants 16 GB or more, and the ~27B brain wants 32 GB or more. windows, mac or linux.


does it need internet
only once, to download the files and the brain you pick. after that, no.


what can't it do
no live news and no web. small models forget and make mistakes. it is not a substitute for emergency services.


is it safe
the scripts are plain text you can read before you run them. the brain downloads from its official source, not from this site.
