What setup actually is

Cutting the cookie to your shape.

  1. We talk

    Where your information lives today, what customers ask over and over, what your crew calls you about.

  2. I connect and load it

    Hook it up to your booking app, point-of-sale or spreadsheet. Load your services, prices, hours and rules. Enter the owner and crew numbers.

  3. I test it on your real questions

    Before any customer sees it, it runs against the questions your business actually gets, including people trying to see things they shouldn't.

  4. Two weeks of tuning

    It goes live, I watch every hand-off and fix what's off. The monthly fee covers this kind of care from then on.
