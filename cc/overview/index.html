Your whole business, one text away.

What it is

A text number that knows your business.

It reads what your business already keeps track of: bookings, orders, jobs, who's working, what's been paid. Then it answers questions about it by text. When you'd normally dig through messages, spreadsheets or a booking app, you just ask.

It's the same reason you read the AI summary at the top of a search instead of opening ten websites. You want the answer, not the pile.

Who it talks to

Four kinds of people. One number. Different answers.

The system knows who's texting by their phone number, because your business already has it: on the job application, on the booking, on the order. That number decides what they're allowed to see.

You · the owner

Ask your business anything.

  • "How many jobs today?"
  • "What's today's profit?"
  • "Anything nobody's assigned to?"
  • "What's Marcus got going on?"

Sees everything.

Your crew

Their own day, nothing else.

  • "What's on my schedule?"
  • "Address and gate code for my 2:15?"

Can't see prices, profit, pay, or anyone else's jobs.

Your customers

Their own order, any time of day.

  • "Is my order ready?"
  • "What time is my appointment?"
  • "Who's coming out?"
  • "How much do I owe?"

Can't see anyone else's order, even with the ticket number.

Everyone else

Someone who just found you.

  • "How much is a tune-up?"
  • "Are you open Sunday?"
  • "Can I book Thursday at 3?"

Only what's on your website. Booking requests go to you.

Privacy

The AI never knows who anyone is.

The thinking is done by a rented AI model, the same kind the big companies use. Before anything reaches it, every name, number, address and code is swapped for a placeholder on hardware I own. It answers blind. The real details are put back on my box, and then the reply goes out.

Fits any business

A cookie cutter that changes shape.

The core is the same everywhere: one number, four kinds of people, privacy first. What changes is what it knows. A dive shop's "order" is a regulator service. A sandwich shop's is a catering tray. An HVAC company's is a 2:15 service call. A salon's is a Thursday color appointment.
