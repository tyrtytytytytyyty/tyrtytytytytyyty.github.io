Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026 · Last updated November 1, 2026

This site (tyycc.com) and the projects on it are run by Tyrese Chatman, a 17-year-old maker. This policy covers the website, GymBuddy (an AI fitness coach you text over iMessage), e (a free offline-AI download at tyycc.com/e), and the Second Brain guide. It explains what is collected, why, and the choices you have. Short version: we only collect what you give us, we don't sell it, and you can ask to be removed any time.

Website forms: e and the Second Brain guide

  • e (tyycc.com/e): you enter a phone number, an email, or both (either one is enough) to get the download link.
  • Second Brain guide (secondbrain.html): you enter an email to get the guide.
  • What we collect: the phone number and/or email you typed, which page or source it came from (for example "e" or "secondbrain-guide"), the page address, and the date and time you submitted.
  • How it's handled: both forms send your submission through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-forwarding service, which emails it to Tyrese's inbox. We don't run a separate database for these.
  • Why: to give you the link or guide, and to follow up about e by text or email. That is exactly what the e form says when you submit: "by sending, you agree we may follow up about e by text or email. no spam. reply STOP anytime." The Second Brain form says: "No spam. Just the guide + updates when I ship something big." We don't promise anything beyond that.
  • Getting removed: reply STOP to any text, or email tyc009@yahoo.com and your info will be deleted from the inbox it landed in.
  • Retention: we keep a form submission until you ask to be removed, or until it is no longer useful for following up, whichever comes first.
  • Your browser: after you submit, a small flag ("e_unlocked" or "sb_unlocked") is saved on your device so the page shows your download without asking again. It holds no personal info.

1. What we collect

  • Profile info — your name, phone number, age, height, weight, goals, and any fitness history, limits, or injuries you share while setting up.
  • Your messages and photos — everything you send to GymBuddy: texts, food photos, workout videos, and progress photos.
  • Activity logs — workouts, food/macro logs, weigh-ins, and chat history.
  • Payment info — handled entirely by Stripe. We receive confirmation and your name after a payment; we never see or store your card number.
  • Website analytics — on the GymBuddy page (gymbuddy-info.html), where collection is enabled, page visits, selected UTM campaign tags, the referring site or app (for example, Instagram), device type (phone or desktop), time spent on the page, how far you scroll, walkthrough progress, button and checkout-link clicks, an anonymous browser-session ID, and an anonymous random browser ID stored on your device so a repeat visit can be counted as returning. We do not store a name, phone number, raw IP address, or message content in the analytics records.

2. How we use it

  • To coach you: personalized training, nutrition, and recovery advice.
  • To log and track your progress over time.
  • To process photos (food photos → macro estimate; workout videos → form check).
  • To understand which campaign links lead to GymBuddy page visits, checkout clicks, and completed subscriptions.
  • To run, maintain, and improve the service.

3. Third parties

  • FormSubmit — forwards the e and Second Brain form submissions to Tyrese's email. Subject to FormSubmit's own privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages — hosts this website, so it receives standard server logs (like IP address) when you load a page. We don't control or see those logs.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the GymBuddy page's anonymous analytics database (see section 4).
  • Google Fonts and cdnjs — pages load fonts from Google Fonts, and the e page loads a graphics library (three.js) from cdnjs, so those providers may see your IP address when the page loads.
  • Stripe — payments and billing. Card data is subject to Stripe's own privacy policy, never ours.
  • AI providers — messages and photos are sent to trusted third-party AI providers (and the models they run) to generate responses. The specific providers and models in use can change from time to time; on any material change we'll update this policy, and such a change may affect response quality, speed, or cost.
  • Apple / iMessage — the messaging transport GymBuddy rides on.

4. Storage & security

The rest of the site (the homepage, e, and the Second Brain page) has no analytics database. The homepage and Second Brain page load the same analytics script, but without the GymBuddy page's collection address it only keeps events in your own browser and sends nothing out. The e page doesn't load it at all. Clicks on tracked links are never tied to your name, phone number, or email.

GymBuddy customer records are stored locally on Tyrese's Mac. Anonymous website event records are stored separately in a private analytics database hosted on Cloudflare and are retained for 180 days. Reasonable safeguards are in place, but no service or internet-connected system is 100% secure.

5. No selling, no ads

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising, and we do not run third-party ads. Your data is used only to run and improve GymBuddy.

6. Retention

GymBuddy customer data is kept while you remain a user and deleted when you request it. Anonymous website analytics events are automatically deleted after 180 days.

7. Your choices & rights

  • Delete your data — text "delete my data" to GymBuddy (or reach Tyrese directly) and your information will be removed. For website form submissions, reply STOP to a text or email tyc009@yahoo.com.
  • Cancel anytime — cancel your subscription by texting "cancel" to GymBuddy.
  • Ask questions — reply to any GymBuddy message with "ty-" at the start and it routes to Tyrese, or contact him directly.

8. Children

This site is run by a 17-year-old and is not directed at children under 13. GymBuddy is for users 13 and older, and nobody under 13 should submit a form on this site. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13; if we find we have, we'll delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date, and material changes will be shared through the service.

10. Contact

Email tyc009@yahoo.com, or, if you're a GymBuddy user, text "ty-" followed by your message to any GymBuddy thread.
